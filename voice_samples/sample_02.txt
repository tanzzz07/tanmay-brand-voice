I think becoming better at something takes more time than people usually expect. We see the final result, but we don't see all the small mistakes and failures that happened before it. Sometimes you work really hard and still don't get the result you wanted. That can be frustrating, you know? But I think that's also where you learn the most. You understand what went wrong, make some changes, and try again. Honestly, I don't think having a perfect routine is necessary. Some days are productive, some days are not, and that's completely normal, you know? The important thing is that you don't completely give up when things don't go according to plan. If I have a bad day, then I try not to overthink it. I just start again from the next day and continue from there.
