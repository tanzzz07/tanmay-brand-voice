I don't think being busy always means that you are being productive. Sometimes you can spend the entire day doing small things and still feel like you have not actually achieved anything. For me, it's more important to know what actually needs to be done. If something is important, I would rather spend a couple of hours focusing on that than spend the whole day switching between different tasks. And obviously sometimes you cannot control everything. Things come up, plans change, and you have to deal with them. So I think having a flexible routine is probably more realistic than trying to follow a perfect schedule every day.
